# reason. Blank lines and lines starting with "#" are ignored.

#     table): the files marked "t.c" below.

#     UPDATE or DELETE, a query) runs together with the " ctx" line after
#     it: the files marked "ctx" below.

#     reads as the variable x. Fixed to (enlist `$"x"). It is in 26

#     the expected text (tests/lib/jar_fixes.q) and compare the rest.
