# q lexer differs from Scala ON PURPOSE (see README, "Intentional

# inputs, so test_golden.q skips them. Their behaviour is covered by unit
# tests in test_lexer.q instead.
#
# Format: one path per line, relative to tests/inputs/, then "#" and the
# reason. Blank lines and lines starting with "#" are ignored.
